# Maintenance workflows

## Inbox triage

1. Inventory inbox items and parse metadata without modifying them. Read-only analysis is the default.
2. Search the vault for matching URL, title/aliases, authorship/date, and central concepts.
3. Classify each item as exact duplicate, incremental evidence, new source, conflicting evidence, or insufficiently understood.
4. Preserve or create the source record. Never collapse distinct sources merely because they agree.
5. Draft atomic durable claims only when they are reusable beyond the source summary. Link every material claim to evidence.
6. Add meaningful links and update the narrowest relevant map.
7. Route retention, trust, unresolved conflict, and AI inference decisions to human review.
8. Present the proposed write scope and wait for confirmation.
9. After confirmation, perform only approved writes, update links/index/log, validate structure, and report all mutations.

Never retain passwords, tokens, private keys, or customer-sensitive information. Stop extraction and report the location without reproducing the secret value.

## Gardening

Prefer small, reviewable passes. Find orphan knowledge notes, broken links, duplicate aliases/IDs, stale review items, overloaded notes, and missing source trails. Propose merges before performing them. When splitting a note, preserve redirects or aliases and do not lose history.

Maps are curated navigation, not database dumps. Add a page only when its placement helps a reader understand or find the topic.

## Outputs

Start from verified knowledge where possible. Mark draft or disputed material explicitly. Keep `derived_from` links so readers can reach the claims and their sources. Do not upgrade the underlying notes merely because they were used in an output.
